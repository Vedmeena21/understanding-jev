## What we left out

- Repos under 300 stars (unless official or from a verified maker).
- Old repos renamed to "Jev" after launch. Their stars came from something else.
- Repos with no English docs.
- Tools that create fake accounts or get around TypeSafe's limits.
- Repos that mention Jev once but don't really use it.
